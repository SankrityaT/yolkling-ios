# Screen recording: tap-by-tap

About 4 minutes. No talking. One take, mistakes are fine, do not restart for a fumble.

## Before you press record

1. **Delete Yolkling from your phone**, then reinstall it from TestFlight. Apple wants to
   see a first launch and a fresh account.
2. Settings > Control Centre > add **Screen Recording** if it is not there.
3. Go to the **home screen** and find the Yolkling icon.
4. Swipe down, press the record button, wait for the 3, 2, 1, then swipe the panel away.
5. Wait 2 seconds on the home screen before touching anything.

---

## The take

**1. Tap the Yolkling icon.** Let it launch.

**2. The sign-in screen.** WAIT 4 SECONDS without touching. Both options need to be
readable: "Sign in with Apple" and "look around for a day first".

**3. Tap "Sign in with Apple".** Complete it with Face ID. This is the login flow they
asked for.

**4. Onboarding.** Tap through at a normal pace:
   - "begin"
   - three quiz questions, any answers
   - on customize, tap a different colour, then "continue"
   - "meet them", let the egg hatch fully
   - type a name, tap "that's the one"
   - "sweet" on the gift screen

**5. Health. Tap "connect health".** When the sheet appears, tap **Turn On All**, then
**Allow**. Do not skip this, it is one of the two prompts they named.

**6. Focus. Tap "sounds good".**

**7. Screen Time. Tap "turn it on".** Tap **Continue**, then **Allow With Passcode** if you
have one set, otherwise **Don't Allow** is fine. The prompt appearing is the point.

**8. Closet. Tap "let's go".** You land on Home.

**9. Tutorial.** Tap "next" three or four times until it clears.

**10. Home.** Tap the creature once so it reacts. Then tap **check in**, pick any mood, and
tap through the card if one appears.

**11. THE PURCHASE. This is the most important beat.**
   - Tap the **Shop** tab, scroll to the bottom, tap **yolkling+**
     (on build 3 there is no card in the Shop: use the **You** tab > **yolkling+** instead)
   - WAIT 3 SECONDS on the paywall so both prices are readable
   - Tap **yearly**, then **become a supporter**
   - Let the Apple sheet appear, confirm with Face ID, let it COMPLETE
   - It is free, TestFlight purchases are sandboxed
   - After it closes, WAIT 3 SECONDS so the supporter glow around your creature is visible

**12. Tap the You tab.** Point out nothing, just let it show "you're a supporter".

**13. Tap "manage your support".** The Customer Center opens. Wait 3 seconds so the cancel
option is visible, then close it.

**14. Tap the Friends tab.**
   - If you already have a friend: open them, and show **Block** and **Report**
   - If you have no friends: tap the add-friend / invite screen and show it, then note in
     the reply that blocking and reporting appear on a friend's profile

**15. Tap the You tab, scroll down, tap "delete account".** Let the confirmation appear,
WAIT 3 SECONDS, then **back out**. Do not actually delete.

**16. Swipe up to the home screen.** Stop the recording.
