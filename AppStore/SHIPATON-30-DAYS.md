## Standing rules

- **One ad channel at a time.** At $50 there is no budget for parallel tests and no
  attribution wired, so sequencing IS the attribution: run ASA for a week, read the
  revenue chart, then TikTok, read it again.
- **No MMP, no Meta/TikTok SDK.** Right for real spend, days of work to measure two days
  of ads. Revisit above ~$500/mo.
- **No AdServices attribution either.** It is ATT-exempt and would not make our "no
  tracking" answer false, but for $30 of brand defense it buys nothing we cannot infer,
  and the clean privacy claim is worth more than the data.
- **Faceless creative.** The creature is the star.
- **Paying customers, not installs.** Cheap installs from CPI services are incentivized
  and poison a retention-dependent app.
