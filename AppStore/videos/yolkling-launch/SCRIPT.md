# Narration

Pace 145-155 wpm, room to breathe. Warm, calm, unhurried. No exclamation marks.

---

I checked my screen time one morning. It said six hours.

Every app I had that was meant to help with that was just one more thing to open.
Another streak to keep. Another notification at nine at night.

So I built the opposite.

You answer three questions, and a creature hatches. It is yours, and there is
only one of it.

After that, it runs on your real life. Your steps. Your sleep. The hours you
spend away from your phone.

Live well, and it is bright, and it is bouncing.

Have a bad day, and it does not scold you, and it does not die. It just gets
sleepy, and it waits for you.

There are two hundred and three species to find, and every one of them is drawn
in code. Not a single image file in the whole app.

Yolkling. A pet that grows when you look after yourself.
