## Intent

The demo video for Yolkling's RevenueCat Shipaton 2026 submission. Judges watch
the first two minutes and are not required to install anything, so this video is
most of what they will ever see of the app.

Yolkling is a cozy iOS virtual pet that is fed by your real life: your steps,
your sleep, and the hours you spend off your phone. It never guilts you. A bad
day makes it sleepy, not sick, and it waits for you.

Tone: warm, calm, confident. Apple product-film restraint rather than a hype
reel. Slow deliberate moves, generous negative space, one idea on screen at a
time, type that settles rather than bounces. Never cute-overload, never a feature
list, no exclamation marks.

## Notes

- Hard rule: only show features that exist in the shipped build. Nothing staged,
  nothing reachable only through a developer seam.
- No music. Devpost forbids copyrighted audio and a claim on a judged submission
  is not worth the risk. Captions must carry the whole message silently.
- No third-party trademarks. Avoid "App Store" wordmark in the end card; say
  "free on iPhone".
- Under two minutes, hard. Target 75-90s.
- Real numbers only: 203 named species, 88 cosmetics, 17 room themes, 100 Yolks
  to start. Do not invent figures.
