# Product Hunt: Tuesday Sep 15

## Why that date

Two weeks after release, so the crashes are out, the first ratings exist and 1.1 has
shipped. Fifteen days before Sep 30, so a traffic bump has time to become revenue, which
is the number the Grand Prize shortlists on. Tuesday is a high-traffic day: placing tenth
on a busy day beats placing third on a quiet one.

**Launch at 12:01 AM Pacific.** PH days start then and ranking is cumulative over 24
hours, so launching at 9 AM throws away a third of the day. Arizona is UTC-7 with no DST,
so in September Pacific IS your local time. Midnight, not 3 AM.

Backup: Wed Sep 16. Do not slip past Sep 17, the runway to convert gets too short.

## The assets

| asset | spec | source |
|---|---|---|
| Thumbnail | 240x240 | the creature on cream, no text, it has to read at 40px |
| Gallery | 1270x760, 5-8 | the App Store captures, recomposed landscape |
| Tagline | 60 chars | see below |
| Topics | 3 | Health & Fitness, iOS, Design Tools or Self-improvement |

**Tagline (54/60):**
```
a virtual pet that grows when you take care of yourself
```

Says the whole product in one line and carries the search terms. Do not use "Yolkling is
a..." in the tagline, the name is already displayed above it.

**Gallery order matters more than the count.** First image is the hatch or the creature at
rest. Second is the health connection, because the differentiator has to arrive before
anyone scrolls away. Trading and cards last.

## The first comment is the launch

More people read the maker's first comment than the description. Write it as a person, not
a press release. A rough shape that fits this product:

- why you built it (the real reason, not the market reason)
- the one decision you would defend to a stranger: Yolks cannot be bought at any price,
  and a year of subscribing gives you less than a third of what a year of showing up does
- what is deliberately missing: no ads, no streak guilt, no loot boxes, no feed
- what you want feedback on

Do NOT list features. The gallery does that.

## What to measure

Not upvotes. **Installs and trials on Sep 15-16**, read off App Store Connect and
RevenueCat. Product Hunt is a spike, and what matters is whether the spike converted and
whether any of it stuck a week later. That retention number is worth more in the Shipaton
write-up than the ranking is.
