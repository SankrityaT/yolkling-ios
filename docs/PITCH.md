# Yolkling — elevator pitch

**Locked one-liner (use this verbatim):**

> Yolkling is a virtual pet that grows when you take care of yourself in real life.
> Your steps, sleep, and time off your phone feed your yolk, and you collect new
> yolks and dress them up.

## Notes
- Lead with the hook: real-life self-care (steps / sleep / less screen time) feeds the creature.
- Collection + cosmetics are secondary, mentioned briefly.
- No em dashes. No "loot boxes / gacha / IAP" framing — it's an earned, cozy economy.
- The creature is a "yolk" (brand name); say "yolk" not "pet" where it fits.
