# Retention: the ethical hook (Nir Eyal, inverted)

The tension, stated plainly: the app has to form a habit or it dies (and all this
work is wasted) — but we explicitly do **not** want people glued to it. The
resolution: use Nir Eyal's **Hook Model**, but make every reward point *away from
the screen*. The habit we build is "check in briefly, feel cared-for, go live well,"
never "scroll."

## The Hook (Trigger → Action → Variable Reward → Investment), applied

**1. Trigger**
- External: ONE gentle daily nudge ("[name] is wondering how your day is"), via
  **provisional** notifications (earned quietly, never demanded). Never streak-shame,
  never "you're about to lose X."
- Internal (the real prize): when you feel stressed / low / lonely, you open Yolkling
  to check in. It becomes your self-care ritual. We earn that by being genuinely soothing.

**2. Action** — make it trivially easy: open → see your creature → one tap to check in
or pet. Five seconds.

**3. Variable reward** — the engagement engine, but **warm, not slot-machine:**
- Your creature's reactions vary; it grew overnight from your sleep; **a friend's
  creature visited while you were away** (the strongest one — social surprise); a
  surprise postcard; an occasional bonus; limited/seasonal cosmetics.
- Unpredictable *warmth*, never manipulation, never loss-framing.

**4. Investment** — loads the next trigger and raises switching cost, **honestly:**
- You named it, you customize it, it grows from *your* real wellness, your friends are
  tied to it. The more you invest, the more it's yours (endowment / IKEA — we already
  lean hard here). Leaving means abandoning something you built.

## The inversion that keeps us honest
Every reward is for living well or connecting, not for screen time:
- Focus sessions reward putting the phone **down**.
- Wellness signals (sleep, steps, daylight) grow the creature — earned **off**-screen.
- Social rewards are small acts of care, not feeds / likes / comparison.
- Sessions are short by design; value compounds from brief daily touches, not long ones.

## Retention systems still to build (the trigger + variable-reward layer)
- One gentle daily **trigger** (provisional notification; warm; never shaming).
- The **variable-reward surprises**: friend visits, overnight growth, occasional bonuses,
  postcards, limited cosmetics.
- A **forgiving streak** (rewards presence, never punishes absence).
- **Referral** (bring a friend → both rewarded): virality + the social investment that
  retains hardest.

North star: someone opens it for 30 seconds, feels a little cared-for, puts the phone
down. They come back because it's warm, it's theirs, and their friends are there — not
because we trapped them.
